# CRM OS — Product Content & Microcopy Guidelines

## 1. Brand Tone of Voice

- **Intelligent & Calm**: Never frantic, never hype-driven. Speak like a senior executive operations partner.
- **Transparent & Accountable**: Always disclose data sources, confidence metrics, and permissions. Never present AI outputs as omniscient fact without evidence.
- **Concise & Direct**: Respect user attention. Lead with outcomes and data numbers before prose explanations.

---

## 2. Feature Explanation Framework (WHAT, WHY, HOW, EXAMPLE, ACTION)

For every complex capability (MCP, AI Agents, Workflows), copy follows the 5-point explanation structure:

```
WHAT:    A secure way to connect AI with external tools.
WHY:     Your AI can work across your real-world business systems without manual copy-pasting.
HOW:     CRM OS Core → Security Gateway → MCP Tool → External API → Audit Log.
EXAMPLE: "Find latest customer email regarding proposal terms."
ACTION:  [Connect Integration]
```

---

## 3. Product Terminology Dictionary

- **Customer 360**: An authoritative account profile consolidating contacts, deals, communication timelines, and health scores.
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**: The open standard used to connect AI models with external tools like Gmail, Calendar, and Slack.
- **Agent**: An autonomous background worker specializing in a specific business domain (Sales, Support, Marketing, Finance, Research).
- **Action Approval Center**: The human-in-the-loop governance inbox where high-impact AI proposals are reviewed before database execution.
- **TenantContext**: The security construct ensuring an organization's records are completely isolated from all other companies on the platform.
